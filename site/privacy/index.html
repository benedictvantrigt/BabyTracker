Napchime privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Napchime is a baby tracker for parents. It is built so that your baby's records stay on your phones: there is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible

Napchime is published by ITER IT, Bestevaerstraat 128, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Questions about privacy: info@iter-it.nl.

Data on your phone

Everything you enter (your baby's name and date of birth, feeds, sleep, diapers, tummy time, pumping, temperature, growth, notes and tags) and your settings are stored in a database on your phone. If you don't use partner sync, nothing leaves the phone.

The camera is only used to scan your partner's invite QR code. No pictures are taken or stored.

Partner sync (optional)

When you link a partner's phone, the phones exchange changes through our sync server (the "relay"), which runs on Cloudflare Workers. All records are end-to-end encrypted: they are encrypted on your phone with a family key that only your family's phones have. The relay, and we, cannot read them.

To do its job, the relay stores:

  • for each linked phone: a random public key that identifies it, when it joined, when it last contacted the relay, and, if reminders by push are on, its push token;
  • the encrypted changes, until every phone in the family has received them, after which they are deleted;
  • encrypted reminder texts with the time they are due, until they have been sent;
  • encrypted phone names and encrypted copies of the family key for each phone;
  • a fingerprint of an open invite code, for up to 15 minutes.

Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and keeps technical request logs for a few days to keep the service running and secure.

Push notifications

To wake a partner's phone after a change and to deliver reminders, the relay sends push messages through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). Google receives the phone's push token and the encrypted message; the content stays encrypted.

Who we share data with

We do not sell or share your data. The only services involved are the ones that run the relay and deliver push messages, acting on our behalf: Cloudflare, Inc. and Google LLC (Firebase). Data may be processed outside your country under their standard safeguards.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • On your phone: until you delete entries or uninstall the app.
  • Remove a phone (Settings, partner sync): the relay drops that phone's push token and name, and the family switches to a new key.
  • Delete family (Settings, partner sync): everything about your family is deleted from the relay straight away.
  • Otherwise, relay data is kept while the family uses sync.

Children

Napchime is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. The data about your baby is entered and controlled by you.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and you can object to its use. Because records are end-to-end encrypted, the relay only holds the technical data listed above, and "Delete family" removes it yourself. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
